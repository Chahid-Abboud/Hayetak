## General Guidance

1. What are good recovery tips after a hard workout?  
Example: Prioritize hydration, sleep, a meal with protein and carbs, and light mobility instead of jumping straight into another hard session.

2. What is a balanced dinner?  
Example: A balanced dinner usually includes lean protein, vegetables, a moderate carb source, and a healthy fat.

3. What are healthy high-protein snacks?  
Example: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, boiled eggs, tuna wraps, protein oats, or roasted chickpeas can all work well.

4. What are good pre-workout meals?  
Example: Good pre-workout meals are easy to digest and include carbs plus some protein, like oats with yogurt, toast with eggs, or rice with chicken.

5. What are good post-workout meals?  
Example: Good post-workout meals combine protein and carbs, such as chicken and rice, yogurt with fruit, or eggs with toast and vegetables.

6. How much protein do people generally need?  
Example: Many active adults aim for roughly 1.2-2.0 g of protein per kg of body weight, depending on goal and training load.

7. What are signs of dehydration?  
Example: Common signs include dark urine, dry mouth, headache, dizziness, fatigue, and reduced workout performance.

8. How can I improve sleep for recovery?  
Example: Keep a steady sleep schedule, reduce screen time late at night, avoid heavy meals right before bed, and make your room cool and dark.

9. What causes muscle soreness?  
Example: Muscle soreness usually comes from training stress, especially after new exercises, higher volume, or eccentric work.

10. What does a balanced plate look like?  
Example: A balanced plate often means half vegetables, a quarter protein, and a quarter carbs, with healthy fats added as needed.

11. What are healthy breakfast ideas?  
Example: Eggs with toast, Greek yogurt with berries, overnight oats, or a smoothie with protein and fruit are solid options.

12. What are high-fiber foods?  
Example: Beans, lentils, oats, vegetables, berries, apples, chia seeds, and whole grains are all high-fiber choices.

13. What are good lean protein sources?  
Example: Chicken breast, turkey, tuna, white fish, low-fat yogurt, cottage cheese, and egg whites are common lean proteins.

14. What are good vegetarian protein sources?  
Example: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, lentils, beans, tofu, tempeh, and edamame are good vegetarian protein options.

15. What are healthy Mediterranean meal ideas?  
Example: Grilled fish with vegetables, lentil soup, chicken with olive oil and herbs, Greek salad, and yogurt-based snacks all fit well.

16. What are healthy low-calorie snack ideas?  
Example: Fruit with yogurt, cucumber with labneh, air-popped popcorn, boiled eggs, or berries with cottage cheese are good choices.

17. What are good habits for fat loss?  
Example: Build meals around protein, keep portions consistent, walk daily, sleep enough, and avoid drinking a lot of calories.

18. What are good habits for muscle gain?  
Example: Eat enough calories, hit protein consistently, train progressively, recover well, and keep meals structured instead of random.

19. What is progressive overload?  
Example: Progressive overload means gradually increasing training demand over time by adding reps, sets, weight, or overall difficulty.

20. How often should someone train in a week?  
Example: Many people do well with 3-5 training days per week, depending on goal, recovery, and schedule.

21. What is the difference between protein, carbs, and fat?  
Example: Protein supports repair and recovery, carbs mainly fuel activity, and fat supports hormones, health, and longer-lasting energy.

22. What are some meal prep tips?  
Example: Cook core proteins and carbs in bulk, keep vegetables ready, repeat simple meals, and plan snacks ahead instead of improvising.

23. What is a rest day?  
Example: A rest day is a day with no hard training so your body can recover, adapt, and come back stronger.

24. Why is hydration important?  
Example: Hydration helps performance, recovery, temperature regulation, digestion, and overall energy levels.

25. What are good stretches after training?  
Example: Light mobility for the muscles you trained, plus easy breathing and cooldown walking, is usually better than forcing deep stretches.

26. What should someone eat before cardio?  
Example: A light carb-focused snack with a bit of protein, like toast and yogurt or fruit and oats, often works well before cardio.

27. What makes a good recovery snack?  
Example: A good recovery snack gives you protein and carbs, like yogurt with fruit, milk and oats, or eggs with toast.

28. How do I stay consistent with meal planning?  
Example: Keep meals simple, repeat a few dependable options, prep ahead, and plan around your schedule instead of perfection.

29. How do I build a simple workout routine?  
Example: Start with 3-4 days per week, cover pushing, pulling, legs, and cardio or mobility, and progress a little over time.

30. How do I update my profile in the app?  
Example: Open Settings, go to Profile, update your saved details, and save so the coach can use the latest information.
